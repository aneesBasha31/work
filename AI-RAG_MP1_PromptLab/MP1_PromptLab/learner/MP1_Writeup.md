# MP1 Prompt Lab — Reflection

## 1. Which strategy won?
Structured prompting won on accuracy and judge score. It achieved 2.8/3 mean accuracy and a 4.0 judge score. Zero-shot was cheapest.

## 2. What surprised you?
All strategies had a 1.0 parse rate, and the accuracy and judge scores were tightly clustered.

## 3. Strategy for your capstone domain
I would use structured prompting for field extraction because explicit schemas improve reliability and make downstream validation easier.

## 4. What would you try next?
I would test more varied snippets, improve ambiguous experience requirements, and compare prompts across another model.
